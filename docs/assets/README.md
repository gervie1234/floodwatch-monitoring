# Component photo references

These supplier/manufacturer images are used as attributed component-identification references in the FloodWatch student tutorial. They are not original photographs of an assembled project. Rights remain with the respective owners. Product links, image URLs, fetched price data and access dates are recorded in `sources.json`.

The selected ESP32 variant is a classic 30-pin ESP32-WROOM DevKit with Micro-USB, not a bare module or expansion board. The sensor image shows the board and waterproof probe. The GPS image includes its antenna. The jumper photo shows the male-female variant; the build also needs male-male wires.

The DFRobot image shows the complete KIT0210 solar package, including a 10 W monocrystalline panel. It is a visual panel example only. Its controller has a different battery/output arrangement and is not approved as a direct substitute for the separate USB bank topology. The UGREEN image is representative of a 20,000 mAh bank; pass-through and low-load compatibility have not been established for it.

Photo-associated estimated ranges are generic budget allowances, not quotations for the exact photographed brand. Do not use the lowest product-level price where a variant-specific price is required. In particular, the ESP32 listing includes lower-priced expansion boards and the enclosure listing includes multiple sizes.
